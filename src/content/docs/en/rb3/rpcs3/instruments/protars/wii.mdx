## Instructions
There are two different ways to connect this Pro Guitar. If you don't have the Rock Band Mustang Receiver for PlayStation 3, you will need to use MIDI to USB.  
Pick whichever is the most convenient for you:

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="RB3 Mustang Receiver for Nintendo Wii (Recommended)">
This is the recommended method because the guitar will work as expected and will be wireless.

**You will need:**
* a Nintendo Wii Wireless Pro Guitar receiver to connect to your computer.  
> ![Nintendo Wii Wireless Receiver](/assets/instruments/adapters/wiimustang.png "Nintendo Wii Wireless Pro Guitar Receiver")
FCC ID: `P25SU96563Y3010R`

## Installing
<Steps>
1. First, [**[go to Zadig's website]**](https://zadig.akeo.ie/) and **download the latest version, then open it** up.

2. Click on **`Options`** then **`List All Devices`**.  
    > <Image src={zadiglistall} alt="Zadig showing 'List All Devices' under 'Options' highlighted." />

3. You should now see devices listed. **Switch it to your RB3 Mustang Guitar for PS3**.  
    > <Image src={zadigsel} alt="Zadig showing 'Harmonix RB3 Mustang Guitar for PlayStation® 3' highlighted in the devices listed." />

4. **After selecting the right device, you should see the option to replace the driver.  
_MAKE SURE YOU ARE REPLACING THE DRIVER ONLY MUSTANG GUITAR!!_**  
Click `Replace Driver`.
    > <Image src={zadigreplace} alt="Zadig with 'Replace Driver' highlighted." />

5. A warning will appear.  
**Again, make sure you have selected your RB3 Mustang Guitar for PS3.**  
After you have made sure, **click** **`Yes`**.  
    > <Image src={zadigwarning} alt="Zadig warning the user that they're about to modify a system driver, with 'Yes' highlighted" />

6. It will then install the driver. As the program says, it may take a few minutes.  
    > <Image src={zadigprogress} alt="Zadig in the middle of a driver install." />

7. If everything goes well, you will get this message:  
    > <Image src={zadigdone} alt="Zadig telling the user that the driver was installed successfully with 'Close' highlighted." />

8. **Close Zadig**.

9. Open up Rock Band 3 in RPCS3 and connect the receiver for your RB3 Mustang Guitar for PS3.  
You should see it automatically assign a player number.
    > <Image src={protaron} alt="A Mustang Pro Guitar with the second player LED lit up." />
    Likewise, in Rock Band 3, you will see your RB3 Mustang Guitar ready to join.
    > <Image src={rb3player} alt="Rock Band 3 with a Pro Guitar ready to join." />
</Steps>

<details>
<summary>Uninstalling</summary>
Applying a Zadig driver on your RB3 Mustang Guitar for PS3 will make it only work on applications with passthrough. This may cause issues with Clone Hero or YARG.  
Thankfully, reverting this is very easy!
<Steps>
1. Open the Start menu and search for `Device Manager`. Open it.
    > <Image src={startdevman} alt="Windows' Start menu, with a result of 'Device Manager'." />

2. Scroll down until you find the `Universal Serial Bus devices` category.  
Expand it and right click on your RB3 Mustang Guitar for PS3 you applied the Zadig driver on and then select `Uninstall device`.
    > <Image src={devman} alt="Device Manager, with a right click menu that popped up. The cursor is over 'Uninstall Device.'" />

3. In the next window that pops up, make sure to tick `Attempt to remove the driver for this device.`  
After that, click `Uninstall` and that's it!  
    > <Image src={devmanun} alt="The device uninstall prompt. 'Attempt to remove the driver for this device' is ticked and the cursor is over 'Uninstall.'" />
</Steps>
When you disconnect then reconnect the receiver for your RB3 Mustang Guitar for PS3, it will revert back to the original driver.
</details>

  <TabItem label="Linux">
<Aside>This requires admin/sudo permissions.</Aside>

<Steps>
1. Navigate to `/etc/udev/` in your file browser.  
Enter the `rules.d` folder.  
If it doesn't exist, make it.
    > <Image src={doludev} alt="A file browser in the 'udev' folder, with a cursor highlighting and clicking on a folder called 'rules.d'." />

2. Create a new file.
    > <Image src={dolnewfile} alt="Creating a new empty file within the 'rules.d' folder." />
    Name it `99-ps3-peripherals.rules`.  
    > <Image src={dolps3rules} alt="Naming the new file." />

3. Open the file in a text editor.  
Add an entry for your RB3 Mustang Guitar for PS3:  
    ```
    SUBSYSTEM=="usb", ATTRS{idVendor}=="1bad", ATTRS{idProduct}=="3430", MODE="0666"
    ```
    In the example below, a PS3 Mustang and PS3 Keyboard are being added.  
    When you're done, save your edits to the file.
    > <Image src={kateps3} alt="A text editor showing the '99-ps3-peripherals.rules' file open. There are two entries in the text file." />

4. Run `sudo udevadm control --reload-rules` in a terminal or reboot your system.
    > <Image src={termreloadrules} alt="A terminal with the command typed in." />

5. Disconnect the receiver for your RB3 Mustang Guitar for PS3.

6. Open up Rock Band 3 in RPCS3 and connect the receiver for your RB3 Mustang Guitar for PS3.  
You should see it automatically assign a player number.
    > <Image src={protaron} alt="A Mustang Pro Guitar with the second player LED lit up." />
    Likewise, in Rock Band 3, you will see your RB3 Mustang Guitar for PS3 ready to join.
    > <Image src={rb3player} alt="Rock Band 3 with a Pro Guitar ready to join." />
</Steps>

## Instructions
Since you can't use the dongle, you will need to connect Rock Band 3 Pro Guitar via the MIDI port on the bottom.  
> <Image src={midimustang} alt="A Rock Band 3 Mustang Pro Guitar showing a 5-DIN MIDI output highlighted in blue with a dotted white outline." />

**For this, you will need a MIDI to USB interface**.  
Here’s an example of a MIDI to USB interface. Most will come with an LED indicator to show activity. **To check that you’ve plugged it in correctly, you should see “MIDI In” blinking when you press a key**.  
> <Image src={miditousb} alt="A MIDI to USB interface." />

**If you have an audio interface, you may already have a way to plug in MIDI** to your computer, as some audio interfaces come with MIDI inputs. For example, this Scarlett has MIDI connections in the back.  
> <Image src={midifs} alt="The back of a Focusrite Scarlett showing a USB port, and 5-DIN MIDI input and output." />

After that, **right click on Rock Band 3** in RPCS3, **then click on `Change Custom Configuration`**.  
> <Image src={iog} alt="Rock Band 3's I/O custom settings showing Emulated MIDI Devices, device type, and device selection highlighted in tan with a solid outline." />

* ![A tan square with a solid outline](/assets/rb3pc/config/smalltan.png "Tan Square"):  
    * 🎸 **Change your `Emulated MIDI type` from `Keyboard` to `Guitar (17 Frets)` if you have a Mustang Pro Guitar, or `Guitar (22 Frets)` if you have a Squier Pro Guitar, then select your MIDI to USB interface in the drop-down menu next to it.**
